# bright-coffee-shop-case-study
Project: Bright Coffee Shop Case Study

Course Provider: BrightLearn

Overview

The Bright Coffee Shop Sales Analysis is a Brightlearn Data Science project completed as part of the BrightLearn Case Study. The project focuses on analysing coffee shop data to identify sales trends, evaluate product performance, and discover opportunities to improve revenue.

The business aims to understand which products generate the most revenue, which time and day intervals experience the highest sales, and which products perform well or require improvement. The goal is to transform raw transactional data into actionable insights that support informed, data-driven business decisions.


Project Objectives

Analyse total revenue and quantities sold

Identify the best-performing and underperforming products

Determine peak sales periods throughout the day

Explore sales trends across product categories and time intervals

Provide recommendations to improve sales performance and revenue growth


Process

The project follows a structured data analysis workflow:

Step 1: Data Preparation by converting the original dataset into a CVS or Excel format and importing the dataset into Databricks for processing and analysis.

Step 2: Data cleaning and transformation

Step 3: Data analysis by using SQL queries in Databricks
Examine sales quantities and identify high-performing and low-performing products.
Compare sales performance across different time intervals.

Step 4: Data visualisation using platforms such as Databricks, Lovable, PowwerBI, Datastudio and Excel.
Use Microsoft Excel pivot tables and charts to summarise sales performance.

Step 5: Presentation
Present your findings by identifying the most profitable revenue-generating products based on sales revenue, determining peak and slower sales periods and recommending strategies for business improvement.


Summary

This project demonstrates how data analytics can help a business better understand its sales performance and identify opportunities for improvement. By cleaning, transforming, analysing, and visualising transactional data, the project aims to provide meaningful insights into revenue, product demand, and sales patterns throughout the day.

The project also demonstrates practical skills in SQL, data preparation, data visualisation, and communicating analytical findings to business stakeholders.
